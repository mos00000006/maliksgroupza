# Controlled hand-swipe flipbook — 13 October 2026

The Wholesale Division leaflet now follows the user's finger/mouse while dragging instead of turning as soon as a short swipe is detected.

## Behaviour
- Hold/touch the leaflet and drag left or right.
- The turning page follows the hand continuously.
- Release after roughly 45% of the page travel to complete the page turn.
- Release before that point and the page smoothly snaps back without changing page.
- Works with touch, stylus and mouse/trackpad pointer dragging.
- Previous/Next buttons, keyboard arrows, thumbnails and full screen remain available.
- No database migration is required.
