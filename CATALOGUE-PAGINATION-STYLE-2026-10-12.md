# Catalogue pagination style update — 12 October 2026

The Company Hub catalogue now uses compact PowerBuild-styled pagination:
- left/right arrow navigation
- direct clickable page numbers
- first and last page kept accessible
- ellipsis used for long page ranges
- active page highlighted in PowerBuild navy with yellow accent
- mobile layout kept compact and horizontally safe

Examples:
- Early pages: ← 1 2 3 4 5 … 42 →
- Middle pages: ← 1 … 19 20 21 … 42 →
- Final pages: ← 1 … 38 39 40 41 42 →
