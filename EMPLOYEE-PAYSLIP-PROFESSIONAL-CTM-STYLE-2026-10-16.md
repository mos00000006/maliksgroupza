# Employee Payslip - Professional Corporate Restyle (2026-10-16)

The PowerBuild payslip PDF has been redesigned into a cleaner, more corporate payroll-document layout inspired by the professional retail payroll style the user requested.

## Design changes
- Clean white A4 document with minimal PowerBuild navy/yellow branding.
- Smaller, professional logo/header instead of a large promotional block.
- Clear payslip number, pay period, payment date and payment method.
- Structured employee-information table.
- Standard payroll breakdown table with Description, Qty/Hours, Rate, Earnings and Deductions.
- Deductions shown clearly in a separate amount column.
- Gross Earnings, Total Deductions and Net Pay summary cards.
- Professional Payment & Payroll Notes section.
- Confidential payroll footer.
- Print-friendly, office-document appearance suitable for filing and emailing.

## File changed
- `app/employee-payslip-pdf.ts`

No database migration is required.
