# Catalogue card uniform styling — 13 October 2026

Updated the catalogue cards so mixed products such as paints, waterproofing and assorted items display in a cleaner and more uniform way.

## What changed
- Standardised card title height to two lines.
- Standardised description height to two lines.
- Cleaned the assorted-product text so it displays shorter, tidier wording.
- Increased the card body height so buttons and badges align better.
- Improved mobile spacing so short and long product names still look even.

- `app/catalogue.tsx`
- `app/globals.css`
- `app/mobile-improvements.css`
