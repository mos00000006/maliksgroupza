# Buyer Workspace — Full Company Visibility

- Buyer accounts continue to see Buyer Workspace and all branch stock orders.
- Any active Hub user assigned **Access Scope: Full company** can now also open Buyer Workspace and see all branch stock orders across every branch.
- Full-company viewers are read-only for normal buying changes; Buyer / procurement administrators retain buying controls.
- Existing approval rights remain unchanged for authorised approvers.
- No database migration is required.
