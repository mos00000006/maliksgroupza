MALIKS HUB - CONAN HAND TOOLS PATCH

1. Extract this ZIP into your existing maliksgroupza project folder.
   The SQL file must land in your existing drizzle folder.
2. In the project terminal run:

   npm run db:migrate:production

3. Refresh Our Catalogue and select Tools, or search Conan.
No front-end deployment is required.

Adds 94 items from all 53 product pages across six Hand Tools pages.
Sizes/models have separate records. Manufacturer codes are used for 72
items. For 22 sizes without published manufacturer codes (bolt cutters
and chisels), descriptive internal CONAN- codes are explicitly identified
in their descriptions. Product pictures use the existing external-image proxy.
Some items share a source range picture; these are not guaranteed to be
individual variant photographs.

Expected catalogue total after kitchen, decor and this patch: 2,112.
Actual additions may differ if your live database has additional products.
Existing items and prices are preserved. New online channels are disabled
with zero prices until you price and enable them.

Validation: executed against the supplied catalogue migrations plus both
earlier patches in SQLite. Verified 94 additions, unique codes, safe repeat
application, matching-name duplicate protection, manual record preservation,
existing pricing preservation and disabled/zero-price new channel settings.

This patch has not been applied to your live database.
