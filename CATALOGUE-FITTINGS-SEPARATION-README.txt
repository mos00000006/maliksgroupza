MALIKS GROUP HUB — CATALOGUE FITTING FAMILY SEPARATION

This patch changes only the wholesale downloadable PDF and interactive page-turn catalogue (leaflet).
It does NOT change saved database products, inventory codes, image references, prices, or other Hub modules.

Sections appear separately:
- Underground (UG)
- Soil & Vent (SV)
- HDPE & Compression Fittings
- Copper & Brass Fittings
- Galvanised Fittings
- PVC & Pressure Fittings
- Waste & Trap Fittings
- Fittings (other/unidentified fittings)

The existing Plumbing section continues to contain products that are not fitting types.
Products are classified by their names/descriptions and context; short prefixes are kept distinct.
This does not reclassify products in the online catalogue's category filter or change database categories.

INSTALL: Copy app/catalogue-pdf-grouping.ts and app/catalogue-pdf.ts into the identical paths in your current Hub, then redeploy. No migration required.
